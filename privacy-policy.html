About this policy

This policy describes information related to visits to the 513 LENSWORKS LLC website. The site is a static information and booking-contact site. It does not have user accounts, website forms, or online checkout.

Analytics and advertising

Our public marketing pages use Google Analytics and Google Ads tags to measure website activity and interactions with contact links. Google may receive information such as page visits, link interactions, browser and device details, and approximate location. Google technologies may use cookies or similar identifiers, depending on the service and settings.

Google explains its data practices in its Privacy Policy and its Analytics privacy disclosures.

Calls and text messages

If you choose to call or text us, information you provide is handled through your phone, carrier, and messaging service. Share only the information needed to discuss your vehicle and requested service. This website does not send your message for you.

Third-party links and choices

The site links to third-party services, including Google Reviews. Those services have their own privacy policies and settings.

You can manage cookies through your browser, use the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on, or review Google's ad settings.

Questions

For questions about this policy, call or text 513-406-6574.

Acerca de este aviso

Este aviso describe la información relacionada con las visitas al sitio web de 513 LENSWORKS LLC. El sitio ofrece información y datos de contacto para reservar. No tiene cuentas de usuario, formularios web ni pagos en línea.
