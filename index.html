Our process

Restore · Protect · Renew

Every headlight is inspected first. Exterior oxidation and failed coating are removed, the lens is refined through progressively finer abrasives, and a UV-protective coating is applied.

  1. 1
    Inspect & clean

    We check the lens condition and prepare the surrounding area.

  2. 2

  3. 3
    UV protection

    The restored lens receives a protective clear coating.

  4. 4
    Final inspection

    We check clarity, finish and surrounding surfaces before wrapping up.
